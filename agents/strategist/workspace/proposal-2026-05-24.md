## 6. Risk Officer — Questions You Should Challenge Me On

I identify **5 potential weaknesses** in this proposal:

### 1. **Sell-Into-Weakness Risk on Future FD Liquidation**
The proposal keeps ₹6.75L in FD for "staged deployment," but no concrete deployment schedule is defined. If markets rally sharply in next 3-6 months, user suffers opportunity cost. If markets crash, user benefits, but that's market timing — which contradicts long-term discipline.

**Challenge:** Should we define a **mechanical deployment calendar** (e.g., ₹1L every month for 6 months regardless of market state) to remove discretion?

### 2. **Single ETF Concentration**
NIFTYBEES is proposed at ₹1.5L (15% of portfolio), which is **exactly at the Hard Rule #2 cap**. Any market appreciation in NIFTYBEES before other assets are deployed could breach the 15% limit.

**Challenge:** Should first tranche split equity across 2 instruments (e.g., NIFTYBEES ₹1L + JUNIORBEES ₹50K) to build headroom?

### 3. **Hybrid Fund Selection Lacks Transparency**
ICICI Pru Balanced Advantage Fund is a dynamic allocation fund — its equity exposure can range from 30-80% based on fund manager's valuation model. For a low-risk user, this introduces **hidden volatility**. During bull markets, fund may spike to 80% equity, amplifying drawdowns.

**Challenge:** Should we use a **fixed-allocation hybrid** (e.g., Aggressive Hybrid with mandated 65-80% equity) or a **conservative hybrid** (20-40% equity) for more predictable risk?

### 4. **SIP Amount Leaves ₹15K/Month Unallocated**
Proposal sets SIP at ₹60K/month out of ₹75K investable surplus, leaving ₹15K/month unallocated. Over 12 months, that's ₹1.8L idle cash — opportunity cost in a 30-year horizon.

**Challenge:** Should we allocate the full ₹75K/month to SIP, or is the ₹15K buffer intentional for flexibility (e.g., emergency expenses, tactical deployment)?

### 5. **Market Timing Bias in Phase 1A**
Proposal justifies smaller first tranche due to "CONDITIONAL GO" market state (VIX 17.91, weak breadth). But user has 30-year horizon — short-term VIX levels are **noise**. Are we overthinking entry timing at the expense of time in market?

**Challenge:** Should Phase 1A deploy a larger tranche (e.g., ₹5L instead of ₹3.25L) to get capital working sooner, trusting that 30 years will smooth out entry-point risk?
