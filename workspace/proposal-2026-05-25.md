**The Problem:** BAJAJHFL and PRAJIND are down 44% and 42%. Selling now realizes ₹8,055 + ₹6,265 = ₹14,321 in losses. If the housing finance or infra sectors recover in the next 3-6 months (e.g., rate cuts boost BAJAJHFL, government infra spending revives PRAJ), we miss the bounce.

**Mitigation:** If the user has strong conviction these will recover, Plan B allows a 60-day hold before re-evaluating. But I don't see the catalyst.

**The Problem:** User plan says corpus "must be substantially capital-safe by Q2 2027" (March 2027 = 10 months from now). 52.4% equity means ₹6.99L is still exposed to market risk. A -20% correction (not uncommon in Indian equities) would erase ₹1.40L — half the goal gap cushion.
