By selling MIDCAP and SMALLCAP ETFs today at RSI 65.6 (mid-cap) and during a broad rally, you risk missing a potential continuation of the rally. If small-cap indices surge another 10-15% in the next month (not unprecedented in momentum markets), you will have exited early.

RSI 65.6 is approaching overbought (>70), not safely in accumulation territory (<40). With a 13-month capital-preservation horizon, **avoiding a -20% to -25% small-cap correction is more important than capturing an additional +10-15% upside.** Soft Rule #1 (de-risk into stability) supports this trade-off.

**Mitigation:** The amendment trims 33% of MIDCAP and 32% of SMALLCAP — **not a full exit**. You retain 2,053 MIDCAP units (₹36,995 value) and 650 SMALLCAP units (₹29,582 value), so if the rally continues, you still participate (at reduced exposure). This is a "trim, don't exit" approach that balances risk reduction with opportunity preservation.
