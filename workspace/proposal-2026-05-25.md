## 5. Known Weaknesses (For Risk Officer Review)

1. **Selling BSE and CUMMINSIND into strength — timing risk.** BSE is at +114% and CUMMINSIND at +53%; both could continue rallying if the current "risk-on" market persists. By exiting today, you forego further upside. However, valuation metrics (BSE PE 81.1, RSI 79.6; CUMMINSIND flagged for weak ROE) suggest reversal risk outweighs opportunity. **Mitigation:** Soft Rule #1 (de-risk into stability) prioritizes locking gains over chasing peaks when the goal horizon is short.

2. **Crystallizing large losses on PRAJ and BAJAJHFL without a catalyst for recovery.** You realize ₹14,321 in losses by exiting these positions today. If either stock rebounds in the next 1-2 months, you will have sold at the bottom. Counter-argument: 13-month horizon doesn't allow holding for multi-year turnarounds, and neither stock shows near-term recovery signals in the Analyst's market data. **Mitigation:** Hard Rule #6 (capital preservation priority) and Soft Rule #1 (de-risk vs. hold-for-recovery) justify the exits.

3. **Equity allocation remains high at 55% despite the rebalance.** Moving from 66.5% to 55% is progress, but the user_plan.md states the corpus "must be substantially capital-safe by Q2 2027" (3 months away: March 2027). At 55% equity, a -15% market correction would still erase ₹1.10L from the corpus — meaningful when the goal gap is ₹26.67L. This rebalance is phase one; **a follow-on session in 1-2 months will be required** to continue the glide path toward 70-80% debt/liquid by March 2027. The Risk Officer should flag whether a more aggressive de-risking (e.g., also trimming MIDCAP/SMALLCAP ETFs today) is warranted.

4. **No tax-loss harvesting optimization.** The proposal exits PRAJ and BAJAJHFL (losses) in the same session as BSE and CUMMINSIND (gains), which offsets taxable gains but doesn't maximize the benefit. If the exits were staged across two financial years, the losses could offset gains in separate years, potentially lowering overall tax. However, Soft Rule #4 (tax awareness) is subordinate to Hard Rule #3 (liquidity / capital-safe glide path) — waiting for tax optimization conflicts with the immediate liquidity deficiency.
