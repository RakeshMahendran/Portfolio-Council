## 5. Adversarial Concerns (v2 Review — Required Even on APPROVE)

### Concern 1: Tranche 3 Binary Trigger May Be Too Rigid
v2 specifies Tranche 3 deployment (₹5L) conditional on "VIX < 16 AND breadth >14/20 green". This is a binary TRUE/FALSE gate.

**Problem:** What if on 2026-07-24:
- VIX = 15.8 (meets threshold by 0.2 points)
- Breadth = 13/20 green (misses by 1 stock)

Do we:
- (A) Strictly enforce "AND" → VETO deployment → retain ₹5L FD
- (B) Exercise judgment → "close enough" → proceed

**Risk:** Binary triggers eliminate judgment. Markets don't have sharp edges — VIX at 16.1 vs 15.9 is noise, not signal.

**Mitigation:** Strategist flags this in Section 6 of v2 ("Need clarification"). Execution Agent should interpret Tranche 3 condition as **guideline, not hard gate**. If both metrics are within 10% of threshold (VIX <17.6, breadth >12.6/20), deploy partial tranche (e.g., ₹2.5L instead of ₹5L).

**Recommendation:** Approve with instruction to Execution: "Tranche 3 condition is directional guidance; use 10% tolerance band, not binary gate."

### Concern 2: ICICI Pru BAF May Face Redemption Pressure in Crash
v2 deploys ₹75K (Tranche 1) + ₹75K (Tranche 2) + ₹25K/month SIP = ₹3.75L into ICICI Pru BAF by month 12.

**Risk:** If market crashes -30% in next 6 months, balanced advantage funds often see panic redemptions → fund manager forced to sell into falling market → amplifies losses. ICICI Pru BAF has ₹45K Cr AUM (healthy), but redemption risk is never zero.

**Historical precedent:** March 2020 — several BAFs experienced 1-2 week NAV calculation suspensions due to illiquid bond holdings amid redemption surge.

**Mitigation:** ₹3.75L is 8.3% of ₹45K Cr AUM (negligible). User's position is tiny vs fund size. But IF ICICI Pru BAF suspends redemptions in a crisis, user's "liquid" hybrid allocation becomes temporarily illiquid.

**Recommendation:** Approve, but note in execution report: "Hybrid fund liquidity is 'T+3 days under normal conditions, up to 2 weeks under stress.' Do not rely on BAF for emergency liquidity — LIQUIDBEES serves that purpose."

### Concern 3: Phased Deployment Sacrifices Compounding If Market Rallies
By deploying only ₹2.5L today (25%) instead of ₹5L (50%), v2 keeps ₹2.5L in FD earning ~6.5% vs potential 10-12% equity returns.

**Opportunity cost calculation:** If Nifty rallies +12% over next 60 days (not implausible — May-July is historically positive seasonally):
- v1 (₹5L deployed): ₹1.5L equity gains 12% = ₹18K gain
- v2 (₹2.5L deployed): ₹0.75L equity gains 12% = ₹9K gain
- **Foregone gain: ₹9K in 60 days**

Over 30 years, repeated conservative timing could cost ₹5-10L in terminal value.

**Counter:** User has low risk tolerance. Psychological safety > ₹9K. And market could equally well drop -10% in next 60 days, in which case v2 outperforms by ₹9K.

**Recommendation:** Approve. This is the explicit trade-off disclosed in Section 5 of v2. User is paying for safety.
