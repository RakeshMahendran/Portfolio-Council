# Portfolio Council — RULES

Derived from `memory/user_plan.md`. Risk Officer enforces.

> **Demo data** — these rules are derived from a sample plan. Modify your real plan via `/profile` or re-run onboarding.

## Hard Rules (Risk MUST veto any violation)

1. **Goal commitment** — every proposal must show net impact toward ₹40,00,000 by May 2027 (24 months).

2. **Concentration cap** — no single position above 15% of total portfolio value. Current portfolio is ₹8,00,000; no stock should exceed ₹1,20,000 in value.

3. **Liquidity buffer** — maintain minimum ₹1,95,000 in cash/liquid instruments (3× monthly outflows of ₹65,000). This is non-negotiable for family financial security.

4. **No cryptocurrency** — zero allocation to crypto assets or tokens.

5. **No forex trading** — no direct currency speculation.

6. **No F&O** — no futures, options, or derivatives.

7. **No penny stocks** — avoid stocks below ₹50 per share or with market cap below ₹500 crore.

8. **No illiquid small-caps** — only invest in stocks with minimum average daily trading volume of ₹5 crore.

9. **Debt quality floor** — any debt/bond investments must be rated AA- or higher; prefer government securities given the moderate horizon.

10. **Emergency fund protection** — the cash reserve is sacred. Investment recommendations must come from new monthly savings (₹1,35,000/month) or rebalancing existing stock portfolio, NOT from cash reserves.

## Soft Rules (prefer; override only with documented justification)

1. **Sector diversification** — maintain exposure across at least 5 different sectors.

2. **Large-cap bias** — given the 24-month horizon, prefer large-cap stocks (top 100 by market cap) for stability. Mid-caps acceptable but should not exceed 30% of equity allocation.

3. **Rebalancing threshold** — consider rebalancing when any position drifts beyond ±20% of target allocation.

4. **Incremental deployment** — deploy monthly savings (₹1,35,000) systematically rather than lump sum, to reduce timing risk.

5. **Exit discipline** — set stop-losses at 15% below purchase price for new positions; trail stops upward as positions gain.

6. **Earnings quality** — prefer companies with consistent cash flow generation and reasonable P/E ratios (<30 for most sectors).

## Required Process

1. Every session must run: Analyst → Strategist → Risk → (if approved) Execution → commit
2. Every proposal must cite specific Hard/Soft rules
3. Risk Officer must offer a "Plan B" alongside any VETO
4. Recovery simulation must be run on any rebalance that moves >5% of portfolio value
5. Goal progress must be reported in every session report
